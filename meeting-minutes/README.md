# Meeting minutes

Space Grade Linux keeps minutes for two meeting series, and publishes both here.

| Series | Directory | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Project meeting (public) | [project/](project/) | Monthly |
| Technical Steering Committee (TSC) meeting | [tsc/](tsc/) | Set by the TSC |

The project meeting is where proposals, including RFCs in Draft, are discussed in the open. The TSC meeting is where the TSC takes up decisions under the [Technical Charter](../CHARTER.md).

## History

Project meeting minutes from January 2025 to August 2026 were published in [elisa-tech/sig-sgl](https://github.com/elisa-tech/sig-sgl) while the project met as the ELISA Space Grade Linux Special Interest Group. They were imported here unchanged, with their history, so their file names and headers still carry the ELISA SIG labels.

## Naming

New minutes are named by series and meeting date:

- `project/SGL-Project-YYYYMMDD.md`
- `tsc/SGL-TSC-YYYYMMDD.md`

The project meeting template is [project/meeting-template.md](project/meeting-template.md).
